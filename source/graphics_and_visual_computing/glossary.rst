Glossary
========


.. glossary::

    Asymmetrical Balance
      A design principle where both sides of an image or layout feel equal in visual weight even though the elements do not match.


    Balance
      The distribution of the visual weight of objects, colors, texture, and space.


    Color Theory
      The study of how colors work together and how they can be used to communicate ideas, emotions, and visual messages.


    Cool Colors
      Colors that are commonly associated with calmness, relaxation, freshness, and stability. They can make a design feel peaceful, professional, clean, or trustworthy.


    Design Principles
      The starting point of visual design. They act as the foundation or building blocks that guide us in creating effective and appealing visuals.

    
    Emphasis
      Highlights the viewer's attention to the most important or specific design element of the layout.


    Graphics and Visual Computing
      A branch of computer science that deals with the creation, processing, analysis, and rendering of digital images and 3D models using computers.


    Hierarchy
      Arranging and emphasizing visual elements in order of their importance.


    Layout and Composition
      The ways visual elements such as text, images, shapes, colors, and empty spaces are arranged in a design.


    Movement
      Guiding the viewer's eye to a predetermined path in the layout.


    Primary Colors
      The basic colors that cannot be created by mixing other colors.


    Proportion
      Creates balance/harmony in terms of size, shape, and scale between visual elements.


    Radial Balance
      A design principle where visual elements are arranged in a circular pattern around a central focal point.


    Repetition
      When reusing the same visual element (color, shape, font, pattern, images, etc.) to create consistency throughout the design.


    Secondary Colors
      Created by mixing two primary colors.


    Symmetrical Balance
      The arrangement of visual elements equally on either side of a central axis, creating a mirror-image effect or equal distribution of visual weight.


    Tertiary Colors
      Created by mixing a primary color with a neighboring secondary color.


    Typography
      The art and technique of arranging and designing text so that it is readable, clear, and visually appealing. In graphic design, typography helps communicate information, establish mood, create visual hierarchy, and strengthen the overall design.


    Variety
      Using different visual elements (colors, shapes, textures, images, etc.) to keep the design interesting.


    Warm Colors
      Colors that are commonly associated with heat, energy, excitement, happiness, and action. They can make a design feel energetic, active, friendly, or attention-grabbing.
