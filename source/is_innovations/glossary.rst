Glossary
========


.. glossary::

    Diode
      An electronic component that acts as a one-way valve for electricity, allowing current to flow in only one direction.

    Desirability
      About creating a user-centered experience, and identifying what your customers really want.

    Disruptive Innovation
      Describes a process where a new, simpler, or more affordable technology and business model takes root at the bottom of a market and eventually displaces established industry leaders.

      Technology or new business model that disrupts the existing market.

    Feasibility
      Focuses entirely on the technical capability to build, scale, and maintain disruptive technology.

    Flex Sensor
      A passive, variable resistor that changes its electrical resistance depending on how much it is bent.

    Incremental Innovation
      Gradual, continuous improvements on existing products and services.

    Information Systems (IS) Innovations
      Focuses on leveraging modern digital tools and computational theories to solve complex business problems, create operational efficiencies, and drive strategic growth.

    Internet of Things
      The network of physical objects or "things" embedded electronics, software, sensors, and network connectivity, which enables these objects to collect and exchange data.

    Jumper Wire
      A short insulated or uninsulated electrical wire with connector pins or sockets at each end, used to connect, configure, or bridge points on a breadboard, Arduino, or microcontroller without soldering.

    Microcontroller
      A compact, self-contained computer on a single integrated circuit (IC) designed to govern specific operations inside embedded systems.

    PESTEL Framework
      A framework or tool used by marketers to analyze and monitor the macro-environmental (external marketing environment) factors that have an impact on an organization, company, or industry. It examines the Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Environmental, and Legal factors in the external environment.

    Photoresistor
      A light-sensitive, analog component whose electrical resistance changes based on the amount of light hitting its surface.

    Piezo Element
      A small electronic component that converts mechanical stress into an electrical signal, or conversely, applies an electrical voltage to produce physical movement or sound waves.

    Push Button
      A momentary tactile switch used as a digital input. It allows a microcontroller to detect user interaction (such as a button press) and trigger actions in code.

    Radical Innovation
      Technological breakthrough that transforms industries, often creates a new market.

    Relay
      An electrically-operated mechanical switch that lets a low-power signal from an Arduino or microcontroller safely control high-voltage or high-current devices.

    Resistor
      A fundamental electronic component that limits or regulates the flow of electrical current in a circuit.

    RGB LED
      A single component that packages three distinct color diodes---red, green, and blue---into one unit to mix and display a full spectrum of colors.

    Soft Potentiometer
      A thing, flexible, touch-sensitive ribbon sensor that functions as a variable resistor or voltage divider based on where you press along its surface.

    Sustaining Innovation
      A significant improvement on a product that aims to sustain the position in an existing market.

    Temperature Sensor
      A linear analog device that measures ambient temperature and converts thermal changes into an electrical voltage signal.

    Transistor
      A miniature semiconductor component that controls the flow of electricity in a circuit.

    Trim Potentiometer
      A small, analog variable resistor used in electronic kits to fine-tune, calibrate, or set voltage levels in a circuit.

    Viability
      Requires you to focus on the value chain in terms of what customers expect from services of your business, looking at both profitability and sustainability to achieve optimal results.
