Design Principles
=================

**Design principles** are the starting point of visual design. They act as the foundation or building blocks that guide us in creating effective and appealing visuals.

Just like an architect studies balance and proportion before constructing a building, a designer must learn these principles first before diving into more advanced visual projects.

They are applied in many fields, but under different names:

- Programming / UI → Design System

- Fashion Design → Style Guide / Collection

- Product Branding → Brand Guidelines

- Web Design → UI Kit / Component Library

- Architecture → Design Standards / Stylebook

- Publishing → Editorial Style Guide

- Marketing → Campaign Guidelines


Design Key Principles
+++++++++++++++++++++


.. grid:: 1
    :gutter: 3

    .. grid-item-card:: Balance

        The distribution of the visual weight of objects, colors, texture, and space.


        .. grid:: 1
            :class-row: surface
            :gutter: 3


            .. grid-item-card:: Symmetrical Balance

                The arrangement of visual elements equally on either side of a central axis, creating a mirror-image effect or equal distribution of visual weight.


            .. grid-item-card:: Asymmetrical Balance

                A design principle where both sides of an image or layout feel equal in visual weight even though the elements do not match.


            .. grid-item-card:: Radial Balance

                A design principle where visual elements are arranged in a circular pattern around a central focal point.


    .. grid-item-card:: Emphasis

        Highlights the viewer's attention to the most important or specific design element of the layout.


    .. grid-item-card:: Movement

        Guiding the viewer's eye to a predetermined path in the layout.


    .. grid-item-card:: Repetition

        When reusing the same visual element (color, shape, font, pattern, images, etc.) to create consistency throughout the design.


    .. grid-item-card:: Proportion

        Creates balance/harmony in terms of size, shape, and scale between visual elements.


    .. grid-item-card:: Hierarchy

        Arranging and emphasizing visual elements in order of their importance.


    .. grid-item-card:: Variety

        Using different visual elements (colors, shapes, textures, images, etc.) to keep the design interesting.
