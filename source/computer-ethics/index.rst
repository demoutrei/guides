Computer Ethics
===============

**Computer ethics** is a set of moral standards and philosophical guidelines that govern the responsible use of computers, digital systems, and technology.


.. admonition:: Core Issues
    :class: tip

    Privacy
      Protecting personal data and preventing unauthorized access to private files or emails.

    Intellectual Property
      Respecting copyrights, patents, and trademarks by avoiding software piracy and plagiarism.

    Security
      Guarding networks and systems against malicious threats like hacking, malware, and data theft.

    Accuracy
      Ensuring the thruthfulness of online information and avoiding the intentional spread of misinformation.

    Social Impact
      Weighing how software design and new technologies affect society, accessibility, and human well-being.
